📸 Best Photo Spots

Instagram-worthy locations for magical vacation photos

📱 Photo Tips

✓ Golden hour is best - 1hr after sunrise, 1hr before sunset

✓ Rope drop = empty backgrounds

✓ PhotoPass photographers know the best angles (free to snap, pay to download)

✓ Memory Maker - unlimited downloads for ~$169

✓ Ask PhotoPass for Magic Shots - they add Disney characters!

✓ Night photos with castle lights = magical

🏰 Magic Kingdom Photo Spots

⭐ Cinderella Castle

  • 📍 Hub (center) - Classic straight-on shot
  • 📍 Main Street USA - Castle framed by buildings
  • 📍 Behind castle (Fantasyland) - Less crowded angle
  • 📍 Wishing Well - With Snow White statue

🎠 Hidden Gems

  • 📍 Purple Wall (Tomorrowland) - Famous Insta spot
