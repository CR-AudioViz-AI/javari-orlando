🆘 What to Do If...

Quick solutions for common park problems

🚨 EMERGENCY

Medical Emergency

Tell any Cast Member/Team Member immediately. Every park has a First Aid station with nurses. For serious emergencies, call 911 or park security.

Lost Child

STAY CALM. Tell the nearest Cast Member immediately. Parks have protocols - children are found quickly. Take a photo of your child each morning showing their outfit!

😰 Common Problems

...Someone in your group gets sick

  • • Go to First Aid (every park has one near the front)
  • • They have OTC meds, bandages, a place to rest
  • • Nurses can assess if you need more care
  • • Buy the park ticket insurance if prone to illness

...It starts raining

  • • Florida rain is usually 30-60 minutes (afternoon storms)
  • • Put on your poncho (bring from home = $1 vs $15 at parks)
  • • Duck into indoor attractions or shops
  • • BONUS: Lines get SHORT after rain - stay and ride!

...A ride breaks down while you're on it

  • • Stay seated and wait for instructions
  • • Cast Members will evacuate if needed (walk-off)
  • • You may get a recovery pass for front-of-line return
  • • It happens! Disney/Universal are pros at handling it

...You lose something

  • • Check Lost & Found at park exit
  • • Disney: DisneyWorld.com/lostandfound (items logged online)
  • • Universal: Visit Guest Services
  • • Items on rides often found during cleaning - check back next day

...Your phone dies

  • • FuelRod kiosks throughout parks - swap dead battery for charged one ($30 first time, $3 swaps)
  • • Some restaurants have outlets
  • • Bring a portable charger! (allowed in parks)

...You get separated from your group

  • • Set a meeting spot before the day (e.g., castle hub, park entrance)
  • • Use Find My Friends or share location
  • • Cast Members can make announcements for families

🍽️ Food & Dining Issues

...You have food allergies

  • • Disney is EXCELLENT with allergies - ask to speak to chef
  • • Note allergies when making dining reservations
  • • Quick service: check allergen menus on kiosks
  • • Carry allergy meds just in case

...You can't get a dining reservation

  • • Check 24-48 hours before - cancellations appear
  • • Use MouseDining.com or Touring Plans for alerts
  • • Walk up at opening - sometimes available
  • • Quick service is just as good (and cheaper!)

...You miss your dining reservation

  • • Disney charges $10/person no-show fee
  • • Cancel at least 24 hours ahead to avoid fee
  • • If running late, call the restaurant ASAP

⚡ Lightning Lane Issues

...Lightning Lane sells out

  • • Book at 7 AM (resort guests) or park open
  • • Have backup ride selections ready
  • • Use standby during off-peak times (rope drop, during fireworks)
  • • Check throughout day - people cancel!

...The line is too long

  • • Check wait times in app - they fluctuate!
  • • Come back later (evening often shorter)
  • • Use Single Rider if available (50%+ shorter)
  • • Rope drop or stay until close for shortest waits

🌦️ Weather Issues

...There's a hurricane warning

  • • Parks close in advance - check official websites
  • • Disney resorts stay open (very safe)
  • • Call to modify/cancel reservations (usually flexible)
  • • Travel insurance covers hurricane closures

...It's too hot to handle

  • • Midday break! Go back to hotel pool 12-4 PM
  • • Indoor attractions: Carousel of Progress, Spaceship Earth, shows
  • • Free ice water at any quick service
  • • Cooling towels, portable fans, misting bottles

👶 Kid-Related Issues

...Your child has a meltdown

  • • It happens to EVERYONE - no judgment
  • • Find a quiet spot (Baby Care centers have quiet rooms)
  • • Ask about sensory break locations
  • • Sometimes you need to leave early - it's okay!

...Your child is too short for a ride

  • • Use Rider Switch - adults take turns
  • • Don't argue with Cast Members (safety rules)
  • • Find age-appropriate alternatives nearby
  • • Check our Height Checker BEFORE visiting

...You run out of diapers/formula

  • • Baby Care Centers sell basics (diapers, formula, wipes)
  • • Located in every park near the front
  • • Also have nursing rooms, changing tables, feeding areas
