🆓 Free Things to Do

Orlando fun that doesn't require park tickets

🛍️ Disney Springs

FREE parking • FREE admission • No park ticket needed

🆓 Completely Free

  • • World of Disney - Largest Disney store on Earth
  • • LEGO Store - Giant LEGO sculptures, play areas
  • • Coca-Cola Store - Rooftop views, Polar Bear meet
  • • Live entertainment - Musicians, performers daily
  • • Marketplace Carousel - Free for kids to watch
  • • Disney PhotoPass - Photos are free, download costs extra

💰 Worth the Splurge

  • • Amphicar boat ride - $125/car, vintage car boats
  • • The Void VR - Star Wars VR experience
  • • Splitsville Bowling - Upscale bowling alley
  • • AMC Disney Springs - Dine-in movie theater
💡 Tip: Disney Springs is open late (usually 11 PM) - great for evenings after parks or rest days!

🎸 Universal CityWalk

FREE after 6 PM parking • FREE admission • No park ticket needed

🆓 Completely Free

  • • Live music - Free concerts on Rising Star stage
  • • Street performers - Entertainment throughout
  • • Universal Studios Store - Browse merch
  • • Vivo shop - Super Nintendo World merchandise
  • • People watching - Lively atmosphere

💰 Worth the Splurge

  • • Toothsome Chocolate Emporium - Incredible milkshakes
  • • Voodoo Doughnut - Weird & wonderful donuts
  • • Hard Rock Cafe - Music memorabilia, live music
  • • Rising Star Karaoke - Sing with live band
💡 Tip: Parking is FREE after 6 PM. Great dinner spot before or after parks!

🏨 Free Disney Resort Activities

You don't have to stay at a resort to explore it! All Disney resorts are open to the public.

Contemporary & Grand Floridian

  • • Watch fireworks from beach
  • • See monorail zoom through lobby
  • • Grand Floridian orchestra (lobby)
  • • Free boat to Magic Kingdom

Polynesian Village

  • • Torch lighting ceremony
  • • Trader Sam's (21+, tiki bar)
  • • Watch fireworks from beach
  • • Volcano fountain

Wilderness Lodge

  • • Stunning Pacific Northwest lobby
  • • Fire Rock Geyser (erupts hourly)
  • • Walking trails
  • • Free boat to Magic Kingdom
💡 How to visit: Park at Disney Springs (free), take a bus to any resort. Or take resort buses/boats between properties!

🏕️ Fort Wilderness (Free!)

  • ✓ Chip 'n' Dale's Campfire Sing-a-Long - FREE! S'mores, characters, outdoor movie
  • ✓ Petting farm - FREE! Horses, donkeys, goats
  • ✓ Electric Water Pageant - Watch from beach (9-10 PM)
  • ✓ Nature trails - Walk through the wilderness
  • 💰 Hoop-Dee-Doo Musical Revue - Hilarious dinner show
  • 💰 Trail rides - Horseback through forest
  • 💰 Archery - Lessons available

🎡 Disney's BoardWalk

Atlantic City-style boardwalk with free entertainment every night!

🆓 Free

  • • Street performers - Magicians, musicians
  • • EPCOT fireworks - Watch from bridge!
  • • Walk to EPCOT - Back entrance (with ticket)
  • • Beautiful evening stroll

💰 Paid

  • • AbracadaBar - Magic-themed cocktail lounge
  • • Atlantic Dance Hall - Nightclub
  • • Jellyrolls - Dueling piano bar (21+)
